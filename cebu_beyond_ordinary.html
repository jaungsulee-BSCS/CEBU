Get to know the island

Where heritage meets the sea.

Cebu is a province in Central Visayas in the Philippines, known for its coastal communities, mountain landscapes, rich history, and vibrant culture. Cebu City serves as a major urban center and gateway to destinations around the province.

Before Spanish colonization, Cebu was an established center of trade. In 1521, Ferdinand Magellan arrived in the island, where the resistance led by Lapulapu at Mactan became an important part of Philippine history. In 1565, the first permanent Spanish settlement in the Philippines was established in Cebu.

Whether you are interested in beaches, heritage, food, or outdoor adventures, Cebu offers experiences for different kinds of travelers.

Taste your way around Cebu.

Cebu's food culture reflects local ingredients and traditions. Here are some specialties and must-try treats.

Cebu Lechon

Slow-roasted whole pig known for crisp skin and fragrant seasoning.

Otap

A crisp, flaky, sugar-coated pastry and popular pasalubong.

Budbod Kabog

A traditional delicacy made with millet and commonly wrapped in banana leaves.

Dried Mangoes

A sweet and chewy Cebu pasalubong favorite.

Getting around & travel tips

  • Arriving: Fly into Mactan-Cebu International Airport. Taxis, ride-hailing services, and transfers connect the airport with Cebu City and Mactan.
  • By land: Buses and vans connect Cebu City with towns in the north and south. Travel times vary with traffic.
  • By sea: Ferries and local boats connect Cebu with nearby islands. Check schedules and sea conditions.
  • Prepare: Bring sun protection, drinking water, cash for small vendors, and a light rain layer.
  • Travel responsibly: Follow local access rules, use authorized guides where required, and avoid disturbing wildlife or coral reefs.

Before you go

Quick travel questions.

What is the best way to get to Cebu?

Many visitors arrive through Mactan-Cebu International Airport. Ferry services also connect Cebu with other islands.

How many days should I spend in Cebu?

A few days can cover selected city attractions, while a longer stay gives you more time to explore destinations in the north and south.

What should I bring?

Bring comfortable clothing, sun protection, reusable water, appropriate footwear, and cash for small local purchases.
